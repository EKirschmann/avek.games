Devlog

Notes from building Glassmarch. It is an early prototype; these are the pieces that moved this month.

Moving to HDRP

We are moving the game from Unity’s URP to HDRP. A trial copy runs the whole campaign route (103 of 103 checks pass on the latest build) with ray-traced shadows and reflections, and a golden-hour light we wanted after seeing it in an islands study. It isn’t finished: the Fenmire mist still costs frames, and the settings menu is only partly built.

Fixes from playtests

We played the whole route on 7 November, and the next day’s fixes came straight from it. In Fenmire, companions could not leave a landing because a 0.64 m step split the walkable ground. Dead trees floated and the lanterns blocked the boardwalk. Thornmarch’s arrival point sat below the level’s fall-out line, so the player respawned every frame. Now every arrival checks for ground, a walkable slope and room for a body before it places you.

Built for a party

Glassmarch is co-op first: one to four players in one shared world, with the host running the fights so everyone sees the same blow land. This week the enemies started scaling with the party. A full group of four meets tougher packs, hired companions and standing summons count for part of a player, and a solo player gets exactly the game as tuned.

The gate network

Every gate on the Glass Road has a twin. Fit a shard into one ring and its partner, far away, flares for a few seconds on its light and goes dark again until you find its own missing glass. So far the route has two pairs, from Hearthvale’s Sword Gate to the Drowned Gate in Fenmire, and from the Sunken Vault’s Twin Gate to the Buried Gate under Thornmarch. Rumours of more are already written into the gate map.
