Your data and how to delete it

Last reviewed November 3, 2026, for Thornmarch 0.1.1.

Thornmarch has no account system and no server. The only thing it keeps is your settings, in a file on your own phone. We never receive it, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf.

What the game stores, on your device

Sound volume, screen shake, damage numbers, joystick size and the frame-rate display. A run in progress is not saved; it ends when the app closes.

How to delete it yourself

  • Remove the files: clear the app’s storage in Android settings.
  • Remove everything: uninstall the app.

If you have emailed us

Support correspondence is the only thing we actually hold: your email address and whatever you chose to put in the message. To have it deleted, write to floridaairborne@gmail.com from the same address and say so. You do not need to give a reason.

If this changes

When saved progress, sign-in, cloud saves, advertising or analytics are added, this page and the privacy policy will be updated before the feature ships.
