Your data and how to delete it

Last reviewed November 8, 2026, for Thornmarch 0.3.35.

Thornmarch has no AVEK account system or developer-operated server. It keeps settings and progress on your phone and, when you are signed in to Google Play Games, syncs a copy of your progress with your Play Games account. AVEK cannot retrieve or delete those copies on your behalf.

What the game stores

On your device: settings such as sound volume, screen shake, damage numbers, joystick size and the frame-rate display, plus progress such as gold, keys, gear, relics, sigils, hero appearance, chapters cleared, quests and best results. A run in progress is not saved; it ends when the app closes.

In Google Play Games, while signed in: a copy of your progress through Saved Games. The game merges cloud progress on sign-in and syncs later profile saves. Device settings are not synced.

How to delete it yourself

  • Local settings and progress: clear Thornmarch’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. This does not delete your cloud save.
  • Cloud progress: close the game on your devices, then use Google Play Games settings to delete Thornmarch’s game data. Google’s game-data deletion instructions explain the current steps.
  • Remove both copies: delete the local data on your devices and the Play Games copy before reopening the game. A remaining local or cloud copy can sync again when you play signed in.

If you have emailed us

Support correspondence is the only thing we actually hold: your email address and whatever you chose to put in the message. To have it deleted, write to eli@avek.games from the same address and say so. You do not need to give a reason.

If this changes

If new data handling is added, this page and the privacy policy will be updated to describe it.
