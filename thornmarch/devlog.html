Devlog

Notes from building Thornmarch. It is an early build in internal testing; these are the pieces that moved recently.

Gear that does something

Every named piece of gear now has its own effect, tied to a patron. A Rot Scythe makes rot eat harder, a Bell Sceptre makes arcs hit harder, a Pilgrim’s Token gives a free reroll. At Epic each piece gains a second line, and four Legendary pieces of one patron make a set: that patron’s armour, its colours, and its blessings first.

Five weapons

The bow has company: a staff of bursting fireballs, a sceptre of instant beams, thrown daggers that stick and fly back, and a scythe that cuts out and back. Every patron blessing works with every weapon.

The blacksmith

Three of the same piece, the same rarity, and the smith hammers them into one of the next rarity. The sheet afterwards shows exactly what changed: power, level cap, stat and effect, and anything newly unlocked.

Foes that walk around things

Foes used to run straight at you and press into statues. Each room now builds a map of its floor, pits and cover, and anyone without a clear line walks the shortest way round.

Smaller and calmer

The download went from 132 MB to 77 MB once textures were sized for phone screens, and the menus lost their oversized headings and buttons.
