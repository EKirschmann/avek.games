Thornmarch

Stand your ground, loose your arrows, and bind cursed patrons to your bow.

A dark-fantasy roguelite for Android phones, played in portrait with one thumb. March across ruined platforms floating over an abyss, room by room. You shoot only while you stand still, so every step is a choice between dodging and dealing damage.

Patrons and pacts

Each level brings a choice of three blessings from cursed patrons: Ember’s stacking burn, Briar’s snaring thorns, Rime’s freezes that shatter. Bond with up to three in a run. Any two of them can seal a pact that turns one patron’s power into the other’s trigger.

Status

In development. Not yet available on Google Play.
