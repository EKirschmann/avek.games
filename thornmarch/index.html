Thornmarch

A dark-fantasy roguelite for phones. March through six cursed realms, take the blessings of rival patrons, and build a hero who will not fall the same way twice.

DevlogSupportPrivacyYour data

Thornmarch is a one-thumb action roguelite for Android. Move to dodge, stand still to shoot. Each march is fifteen rooms with a Guardian at the end, and every level-up offers blessings from six patrons: Ember, Briar, Rime, Storm, Blight and Umbral. Mix them, and your arrows burn, snare, freeze, arc, rot or hex. Between runs, gear, relics and sigils carry your progress forward, and the blacksmith forges three of the same piece into something better.

An early build in internal testing on Google Play. No release date yet.

The march

Six chapters, fifteen rooms each, and a Guardian at the end.

  1. The Ruined March

    “Ruins drift in the dark.” Broken walls, deserters with rusty swords, and the Ruin Giant at the end, who charges when he loses patience.

  2. The Thornwood

    “Goblins crawl out of the briars.” They come at you from the side and dart between the roots. The Briar Troll calls more of them when it is hurt.

  3. The Frostfang Pass

    “Wolves howl through the pass.” Wolves circle, crouch and lunge. Hunters loose three arrows at once. The Pass Colossus shields itself and slams the ice.

  4. The Drowned Catacombs

    “The drowned dead rise.” Skeletons plod, then lurch. The Drowned King raises the dead around him.

  5. The Sunken Spires

    “The wrapped dead stir in the spires.” Mummies shamble in slowly and hit hard. The Spire Colossus fires spirals of bolts.

  6. The Ember Deep

    “Demons rise from the heat.” Demons vanish and step out beside you mid-swing. The Ember Behemoth charges, and the ground burns where it lands.
