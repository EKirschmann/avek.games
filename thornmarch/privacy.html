Thornmarch Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This policy covers the Thornmarch game, Android package games.avek.thornmarch, and explains how version 0.3.35 handles information, including optional Google Play Games cloud saving.

Information stored by the game

Thornmarch stores your settings (sound volume, screen shake, damage numbers, joystick size and the frame-rate display) and your game progress (such as gold, keys, gear, relics, sigils, hero appearance, chapters cleared, quest progress and best results) on your device. A run in progress lives only in memory and ends when the app closes. This information is used to run the game. The game does not send it to us.

No game account is required. The game has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, in-app purchases or developer-operated servers. It does not collect or transmit your location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, advertising identifier or gameplay analytics. We do not sell game data.

Engine diagnostics

Thornmarch is built with the Unity engine. When the game starts, the engine sends Unity Technologies basic technical information about the device, such as the device model, operating system version, graphics hardware and screen resolution. It is used in aggregate to understand which devices run the game, is not linked to your identity, is not used for advertising, and is sent over an encrypted connection. Unity handles it under its own privacy policy.

Google Play Games

On Android the game may sign you in to Google Play Games automatically, using the Play Games profile already on your device. Google provides this service under its own privacy policy; we receive only what the Play Games service shares with the game, such as your player ID and gamer name. While you are signed in, the game reads and writes a copy of your progress using Google Play Games Saved Games. It merges saved progress when sign-in succeeds and syncs subsequent profile saves, so progress can follow you across devices. Settings stay on each device. The cloud copy is stored by Google, not on an AVEK server. You can play without signing in; in that case, progress stays on your device. You can manage sign-in and delete your Play Games profile or individual game data through Google Play Games settings.

Retention and deletion

Settings and progress stay on your device until you clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. Deleting local files does not delete the cloud copy in Google Play Games; signed-in play can restore saved progress. AVEK cannot delete that copy on your behalf. Step-by-step instructions are on the Your data page.

Support correspondence

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and investigate reported problems, keep it only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations, and delete it on request. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Messages may be processed by our email provider.

Stores, hosting and external services

Google Play and your device platform handle downloads, updates and their own diagnostics under their privacy policies. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to serve and secure it, under GitHub’s privacy statement. This page embeds no advertising, analytics, forms or third-party fonts.

The separate beta-request page uses Cloudflare for spam protection and request delivery. See website privacy for how requests are handled. Website requests and group membership are separate from the game’s saved progress.

Children and future changes

Thornmarch is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, does not ask for age, name or contact details during play, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

If advertising, developer-run accounts, analytics or other data collection are ever added, we will update this policy and the store disclosures before those features ship, and ask for consent where required. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
