Thornmarch Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This policy covers the Thornmarch game, Android package games.avek.thornmarch, and explains how its current offline gameplay handles information.

Information stored by the game

Thornmarch stores your settings (sound volume, screen shake, damage numbers, joystick size and the frame-rate display) on your device. A run in progress lives only in memory and ends when the app closes. This information is used to run the game. The game does not send it to us.

No game account or sign-in is required. The current game has no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, in-app purchases or developer-operated cloud save service, and no network code of its own. It does not collect or transmit your location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, advertising identifier or gameplay analytics. We do not sell game data.

Retention and deletion

Settings stay on your device until you clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. We cannot retrieve or erase information stored only on your device. Step-by-step instructions are on the your data page.

Support correspondence

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and investigate reported problems, keep it only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations, and delete it on request. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Messages may be processed by our email provider.

Stores, hosting and external services

Google Play and your device platform handle downloads, updates and their own diagnostics under their privacy policies. GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to serve and secure it, under GitHub’s privacy statement. This page embeds no advertising, analytics, forms or third-party fonts.

Children and future changes

Thornmarch is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, does not ask for age, name or contact details during play, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will remove it.

If advertising, online accounts, cloud saving or analytics are ever added, we will update this policy and the store disclosures before those features ship, and ask for consent where required. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.
