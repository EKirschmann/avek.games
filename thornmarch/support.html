The game itself

What do I need to play it?

An Android phone. Thornmarch plays in portrait with one thumb: drag anywhere to move, and let go to stand and shoot.

Why does my hero stop shooting while I move?

That is the heart of the game: you shoot only while standing still. Some blessings change that, such as Skirmisher, which keeps you firing at a slower rate while you move.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. It plays entirely offline, with no account, sign-in or server.

Is there anything to buy?

Not today. There is no advertising and no in-app purchase.

Can I pause?

Yes. Tap the gear in the top-right corner. The game also pauses itself if you leave the app.

Settings

Sound effects volume, screen shake, damage numbers, joystick size and a frame-rate display are on the title screen and in the pause menu. They are remembered between sessions.

Reporting a bug

Email what happened, what you expected, your phone model and the game version (shown on the title screen). A screenshot or a short screen recording helps a lot.
