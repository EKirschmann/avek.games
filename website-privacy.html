Website privacy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This notice covers the AVEK website and beta requests. Each game has its own privacy information; submitting a website request does not change how a game stores your progress.

Beta requests and email

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you provide. The beta form asks which game interests you, your email, an optional device description and message, and confirmation that you are 13 or older and want a reply about your request. We use these details to respond and coordinate possible testing. A request does not automatically enroll you in a test or subscribe you to marketing.

When available, the form is processed by Cloudflare and delivered to our Gmail inbox. Email sent to an @avek.games address may also be forwarded through Cloudflare to Gmail. Those providers process messages to deliver, secure, and operate their services. We do not sell your request details.

Spam protection and hosting

The active request form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to limit automated submissions. Turnstile may process technical information about your browser and network, including your IP address, to perform this check. The form service validates requests and limits repeated submissions. We do not add request contents to application logs or maintain a separate signup database.

GitHub Pages serves this website and may process technical request information under its own privacy statement. We do not use advertising trackers or website analytics. Fonts are hosted with the site. The homepage and game privacy pages do not load the form’s spam-check script.

Retention and deletion

We keep correspondence as needed to handle your request and coordinate testing, and remove it when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable obligations. You can ask us to stop contacting you or delete your correspondence by emailing eli@avek.games. This is separate from leaving a Google Play tester group or deleting a game’s local save.

Children

Beta requests are for people aged 13 and over. Please do not submit details for children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can remove it.
